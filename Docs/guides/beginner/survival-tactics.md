# Your First RimWorld Colony Won't Crash and Burn With These Essential Survival Tactics

## Quick-Start Guide: First 15 Minutes to Survival

When your initial three colonists arrive, execute these steps for immediate survival:

1. **Pause and Assess** – Upon loading, pause the game. Unforbid starting items (marked with red X's). Scan for dangers and identify a suitable base location near ruins or hills with fertile soil nearby.
2. **Equip Weapons** – Draft colonists and assign firearms to those with higher Shooting skill, melee weapons to those with better Melee ability.
3. **Build a Shelter** – Construct a wooden shack approximately 9x9 tiles. Ensure at least one enclosed room with a door.
4. **Stockpile and Roof** – Designate an internal Stockpile Zone and external Dumping Stockpile for rock chunks and corpses. Enclosed structures automatically gain roofs.
5. **Place Beds** – Install beds or sleeping spots for each colonist inside the shelter to avoid mood penalties.
6. **Get Food Production Started** – Locate fertile soil patches and create Growing Zones (6x6 or larger). Plant rice (fastest growth) in one plot and potatoes or corn in another. Harvest wild berry bushes for immediate sustenance.
7. **Establish a Cooking Spot** – Build a campfire or steel stove inside your shelter. Set a cooking bill to "Cook Simple Meal (Do until you have X=10)".
8. **Set Work Priorities** – Enable Manual Priorities in the Work tab. Assign Firefighting, Patient, Bed Rest, Basic, Hauling, and Cleaning as priority 1 for all colonists. Then assign specializations based on individual skills and passions.
9. **Add Basic Comforts** – Build a table with chairs to avoid the "Ate without table" mood debuff (-3). Place a Horseshoes Pin outside for recreation.
10. **Plan a Freezer** – Reserve space for a 6x6 food storage room adjacent to your shelter. Plan for later installation of two coolers once electricity research completes.
11. **Minimal Defenses** – Create sandbag or barricade cover spots outside your shelter, positioned to provide your colonists tactical advantage against approaching raiders.
12. **Schedule & Manage Needs** – Review the Schedule tab. Allocate 1-2 hours of Recreation time to prevent work-related mental breaks.

**Success Marker:** By end of Day 1, you should have shelter with beds, prepared meals, growing crops, and basic defenses.

## Core Survival

### Early Food and Farming Basics

Food scarcity causes most early colony failures. Key strategies:

- **Rationing Survival Meals** – Use packaged meals strategically; they never spoil but shouldn't be consumed early
- **Berry Picking and Hunting** – Harvest wild berries and hunt harmless animals like hares or turkeys
- **Quick Cooking** – Cooked meals reduce food poisoning risk, particularly with cooks at skill level 4+
- **Farming Strategy** – Rice matures in approximately 3 days; potatoes handle poor soil; corn requires 11+ days but yields heavily
- **Hunting Caution** – Focus on non-aggressive animals; draft backup colonists for protection
- **Kitchen Cleanliness** – Maintain sanitary cooking areas to prevent food poisoning incidents
- **Temperature Management** – Build passive coolers in hot biomes; use natural cold in cold biomes

**Community Mantra:** "Keep pawns fed, keep pawns happy, keep pawns protected" – prioritize in this order.

### Shelter, Warmth & Base Setup

- **Barracks vs Bedrooms** – Use shared barracks early for efficiency; transition to private rooms later
- **Minimizing Travel** – Design layouts reducing walking distances between common work areas
- **Combined Spaces** – Merge dining and recreation areas as your base's social hub
- **Using Existing Structures** – Ancient ruins can supplement your base but avoid "Ancient Danger" sealed rooms
- **Climate Control** – Maintain indoor temperatures between 16°C–26°C using campfires (cold) or passive coolers (heat)
- **Lighting** – Install torch lamps indoors to prevent darkness mood penalties and work speed reductions
- **Expandable Design** – Plan initial construction allowing future expansion; comb-shaped bases offer good patterns

### Early Hazards: Disease, Wounds, and Fires

- **Disease (Flu, Malaria)** – Assign sick colonists to medical beds; monitor Immunity percentage vs. disease progression
- **Injuries and Healing** – Rescue downed colonists immediately and tend wounds in clean rooms to reduce infection
- **Fire Management** – Draft colonists to extinguish fires or use Home zones; maintain firebreaks around wooden structures; enable Firefighting priority 1 for all
- **Mental Breaks** – Monitor mood bars continuously; address persistent red or orange indicators before breaks occur

## Base Building Basics

### General Base Design Principles

- **Centralize Common Areas** – Position kitchen, dining, and recreation centrally to reduce walking
- **Think in Zones** – Organize into distinct functional areas
- **Conjoined Workshops** – Place interdependent workstations together for Tool Cabinet sharing and unified climate control
- **Storage Solutions** – Use distributed stockpiles near task locations rather than single central storage; use shelves for organization
- **Power Planning** – Plan generator and conduit placement; hide conduits in walls when possible
- **Avoid Overbuilding Early** – Build only when needed; excess wealth triggers stronger raids
- **Terrain Adaptation** – Use mountains for natural insulation and protection
- **Beautification Impact** – Smooth stone floors, wood floors, and flowering plant pots boost mood

#### Classic Early Base Layout Example

A central 11x11 multi-purpose room containing:
- 3-5 barracks beds in one corner
- Table and chairs for dining
- 2-3 workbenches along walls
- Storage shelves with frequently used items
- 1-2 lamps for lighting

Adjacent: 6x6 kitchen with attached freezer

Nearby: 2-3 bed medical room

Perimeter: Encircling walls with 1-2 defensive choke points

### Useful Structures & When to Build Them

#### Early Priority (Days 1-5)
- Research Bench
- Basic Walls & Doors
- Horseshoes Pin
- Campfire/Stove
- Growing Zones
- Basic Traps

#### Mid Priority (Week 1-2)
- Freezer (post-power generation)
- Power Generation (wood generator or wind turbine)
- Chess Table (recreation variety)
- Expanded Defensive Structures
- Butcher Table
- Stonecutting Table

#### Later Priority (Week 3+)
- Turrets (after Microelectronics research)
- Comms Console
- Private Bedrooms
- Advanced Recreation (TV, billiards)
- Hospital with vitals monitors
- Workshop Expansions

## Defense Basics

### Basic Defensive Tactics

1. **Early Detection** – Pause when raid alerts occur; assess enemy numbers, weapons, and approach direction
2. **Terrain and Cover** – Position shooters behind sandbags, walls, or natural rocks facing enemy approach
3. **Focus Fire** – Concentrate colonist fire on single targets, prioritizing most dangerous enemies
4. **Kiting** – Use fast, ranged colonists to shoot, retreat, and repeat against slow melee enemies
5. **Melee Blocking** – Deploy skilled melee colonists as damage absorbers while others provide ranged support
6. **Tactical Retreat** – Fall back deeper into base when necessary for ambush positions and superior cover

#### Base Defenses You Can Build Early
- **Traps** – Wooden spike traps deal 40 damage, sufficient for many early enemies
- **Choke Points** – Create narrow passages forcing concentrated enemy positioning
- **Defensive Landscaping** – Clear trees and crops to eliminate enemy cover
- **Cover Positions** – Place sandbags and barricades at strategic firing locations

### Advanced Defense: Killboxes and Beyond

A proper killbox typically features:
- Zigzag entrance path with deadfall traps
- Open firing zone with minimal enemy cover
- Sandbag positions for colonist shooters
- Concrete or sterile flooring preventing fires

#### Countering Special Raid Types
- **Sappers** – Will tunnel through walls; requires mobile defense
- **Sieges** – Attack before mortar positioning completes
- **Drop Pods** – Maintain interior defensive positions
- **Mechanoids** – Deploy armor-piercing weapons or concentrated traps

## Colony Management

### Work Priority & Scheduling Tips

- **Priority Numbers** – Assign life-critical tasks (Firefighting, Patient, Doctor) to priority 1; primary roles to 2; secondary to 3; others to 4
- **Task Specialization** – Single-person assignments for key roles like cooking prevent inefficiency
- **Shift Systems** – Assign Night Owl colonists to nighttime work (11pm-6pm sleep), providing 24-hour productivity and mood benefits
- **Joy Balance** – Schedule 1-2 hours Recreation time preventing "Recreation Deprived" debuffs
- **Prevent Idleness** – Enable Hauling and Cleaning for all colonists at some priority
- **Passion Matching** – Assign colonists with passion icons (flame symbols) to related tasks for faster learning and mood bonuses

### Mood Management & Recreation

#### Key Mood Buffs
- Comfortable furniture and quality beds
- Spacious room interiors
- Impressive/beautiful rooms
- Social gatherings and parties
- Recreation variety
- Fine meals (compared to simple meals)

#### Mood Penalties to Avoid
- Hunger or tiredness from neglected basic needs
- Ugly or dirty environments
- Witnessing deaths
- Sleep disturbance from foot traffic
- Eating without tables
- Worn-out apparel (below 50% durability)
- Recreation deprivation

#### Managing Mental Breaks

Break types and responses:
- **Sad Wander** – Generally harmless; allow to pass
- **Berserk** – Subdue using melee weapons (non-lethal)
- **Insulting Spree** – Isolate from other colonists
- **Food/Drug Binge** – Forbid substances if possible
- **Hide in Room** – Monitor for starvation

Post-break, colonists receive "Catharsis" buff (+40 mood).

### Growth: New Recruits and Colony Expansion

#### Recruiting Prisoners
- Capture downed raiders in enclosed prisoner rooms with beds
- Wardens attempt recruitment over time
- Feed and maintain health for improved recruitment chances

#### Optimization Strategy
A five-person colony with highly skilled, contented members outperforms a ten-person colony with poor management. Prioritize quality.

## Beginner FAQ

**Q: What should I research first?**
Prioritize Battery and Solar Panels for power generation and storage. Stonecutting enables durable stone structures. Complex Furniture (tribal start) unlocks better beds. Food refrigeration (Air Conditioning) also deserves early priority.

**Q: How many growers/miners/cooks do I need for X colonists?**
For five colonists, allocate 1 dedicated cook, 1-2 dedicated growers, 1 constructor, 1 researcher, with others cross-trained. One skilled grower manages substantial farms with hauling assistance. One cook sustains approximately 10 colonists' meals if dedicated.

**Q: How do I deal with infestations (bugs) in a mountain base?**
Prevention: Keep mountain rooms small and cold (bugs avoid temperatures below -17°C). Combat: Use fire (molotovs are effective) or kite bugs into chokepoints. Early-game options include shotguns, molotovs, and tactical retreats.

**Q: Should I use turrets or traps or colonists for defense?**
Employ layered defense combining all three. Traps offer early cost-effectiveness. Turrets provide mid-game DPS and draw fire once power is stable. Note: Excess turrets increase raid point scaling.

**Q: When is it safe to take in more colonists?**
Assess food surplus and housing availability first. Each colonist increases workload but adds productivity. Avoid taking refugees if barely sustaining current population.
