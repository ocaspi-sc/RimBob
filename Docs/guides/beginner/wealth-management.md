# The RimWorld Trade Masterclass: Orbital Beacons, Caravan Economics, and Wealth Management

## Understanding RimWorld's Economic Ecosystem

RimWorld features two primary trading systems:

**Orbital Trade Beacon System**
- Requires Microelectronics research, comms console, and beacons
- Items must be within the beacon's 15-tile diameter radius to be available for trade
- Animals are tradeable regardless of beacon range

**Caravan Trading System**
- Allows expeditions with colonists, animals, and prisoners
- No beacon range limitations
- Items leaving the map don't count toward base wealth calculations

---

## The Wealth-Raid Relationship

Colony wealth includes all items on the map at full value, equipped gear and resources, pawns and animals, and structures at half their value.

**Key thresholds:**
- Wealth scales raid difficulty up to 1 million silver
- Can generate up to 4,200 raid points (nearly half the 10k raid point cap on higher difficulties)
- Approximately 80,000–100,000 wealth triggers dramatic raid escalation

**Effective strategies:**
- Keep only essential items visible
- Remove excess wealth via caravans
- Gift unnecessary items to improve faction relations
- Avoid overproduction and stockpiling

## Orbital Trade Beacon Mastery

### Core Principle: Raid Targeting

Approximately 40% of all potential raids will specifically target your most recently-built, powered orbital trade beacon.

### The Decoy Beacon Strategy

1. Build primary beacon first in safe location (oldest beacon = permanent trading post)
2. Construct second beacon in desired killbox/defensive area
3. Keep decoy beacon **unpowered** via switched-off power conduit
4. The newest powered beacon attracts ~40% of raids
5. Flip power switch only when conducting trade to avoid unwanted attacks

### Beacon Specifications
- Effective range: 15-tile diameter
- Designated landing zone should feature clear firing lines, heavy turret coverage, and strategic cover positions

## Caravan Economics: Risk vs Reward

### Optimal Caravan Composition
- **Security Detail:** 2-3 well-equipped, mobile fighters
- **Medical Specialist:** Non-negotiable for treating wounds during travel

### Animal Selection Trade-offs

**Horses**
- 160% speed multiplier for individual colonists
- Additional +60% "all-mounted" bonus when every colonist rides
- Ideal for high-value, low-volume goods or dangerous routes

**Elephants/Muffalo**
- Massive carrying capacity (~140kg in caravans)
- Superior for bulk commodity trading
- Significantly reduced speed increases journey duration and risk

### Supply Logistics
- Each colonist consumes one survival meal approximately every 1.6 days
- Five-person team requires minimum 22 meals for week-long journey
- Medicine: 5-10 units recommended as weight-efficient insurance

**Prisoner Economics:**
- Each requires dedicated guard
- Reduces caravan speed by approximately 6% each
- Only profitable on secure, established routes with overwhelming security

## Advanced Wealth Management Strategies

### Solving the "Silver Ceiling Problem"

**Destruction:** Burn tattered apparel, shattered weapons, and low-quality furniture in a crematorium or campfire. This instantly removes their wealth value.

**Gifting:** Transport pods to allied factions reduce wealth while boosting relations and enabling military support requests during crises.

**Strategic Spending:** Convert raw materials into permanent defensive and productivity enhancements:
- Build superior walls
- Purchase advanced components and bionics
- Invest in masterwork armor and weapons for key colonists

### Production Chain Optimization

**Yayo Production**
- 8 psychoid leaves → 23 silver (2.875 per leaf)
- Wealth-efficient for weight and value; ideal for caravans and trade

**Flake Production**
- 4 psychoid leaves → 14 silver (3.5 per leaf)
- Generates 50% more market value from same raw input
- Heavier and less value-dense than yayo; superior for on-site production

### Hidden Wealth Techniques

**Raw Material Storage**
Raw plasteel, components, and gold are calculated at only 20% of true market value until crafted into items. Maintain large stockpiles as unprocessed materials.

**Animal Management**
- Large herds represent constant wealth accumulation through breeding
- Regularly cull older, non-productive animals
- Sell offspring to control wealth inflation

## Trader Psychology and Manipulation Tactics

### Factional Buying Preferences

**Outlanders**
- Pay premium for advanced components
- Value bionics and complex weapons

**Tribal Factions**
- Highly value medicine, textiles
- Prefer natural materials (wood, leather)

**Pirates**
- Offer best prices for recreational drugs and alcohol
- Wide variety of weaponry available

### Pricing Mechanics: The 60/140 Rule

You will always sell an item for 60% of its market value and buy for no less than 140% (a 140% markup).

**Social Skill Advantage:** Colonists with high Social skill secure deals up to 2% better on prices compared to unskilled traders.

### Timing Strategy
Faction bases operate on a **30-day restock cycle**. Arriving immediately after restock ensures maximum silver availability and full inventory of desired goods.

### Gift Psychology
Pre-transaction generosity — gifting a small portion of your trade goods, especially items the faction desires — provides a substantial relationship boost that applies a hidden discount to all subsequent transactions.

## Practical Wealth Rules

- Monitor wealth graphs in history tab for spikes
- Use bribery and alliances to manage raid frequency
- Gift surplus goods to powerful hostile factions for temporary peace
- Produce only what you need for immediate use, trade, or gifting
- Convert liquid assets like silver into unassailable defensive and diplomatic power
