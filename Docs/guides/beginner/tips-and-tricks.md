# RimWorld Guide: 11 Important Tips and Tricks

## 1. Pick the Right Storyteller

- **Cassandra**: Classic experience with gradual difficulty increase and occasional pauses between events
- **Phoebe**: Best for beginners with longer pauses between events and late-game difficulty increases
- **Randy Random**: For experienced players; unpredictable with challenging early events

## 2. Select Your World and Climate Zone

Create your scenario with a random seed and 30% planet coverage at average temperature and rainfall. Choose temperate or coniferous forests as landing sites near other communities for trading opportunities.

## 3. Assemble Your Crew

Recruit three colonists with complementary skills. Priority skills include:
- Farming and mining for resources and food
- Medicine for treating illness and injuries
- Building for construction
- Shooting for defense

## 4. Take Care of Basic Needs

Ensure colonists have food, shelter with beds, safety, and happiness. Prison facilities are also essential.

## 5. Distribute the Work Properly

Use the Work tab to prioritize tasks (1=very important, 4=unimportant). Assign roles based on skills: medical experts become doctors, skilled shooters become soldiers, farmers prioritize food supply.

## 6. Build in a Safe Place

Settle in mountain-surrounded areas for predictable enemy routes and natural defense. Ensure fertile soil and adequate space for buildings and solar panels.

## 7. Recruit Prisoners

Maintain a prisoner room with a bed. Colonists can recruit prisoners through interaction to expand your population.

## 8. Grow Food

Initial food supplies last approximately five days. Establish stable food production through hunting and farming. Build kitchens, refrigerators, and stoves for food preparation. Research hydroponics for cold-weather farming.

## 9. What Should You Build First?

Recommended construction order:
1. Warehouse and garbage dump
2. Five medium-sized fields (two for cotton/medicine, three for food crops: corn, potatoes, rice)
3. Roofed camp with surrounding walls
4. Dwellings with sleeping areas, holding cell, and sick room
5. At least two solar panels with batteries
6. Cold storage house
7. Kitchen with stove and butcher's bench

## 10. Use the Trash Zone

Designate trash zones to improve settlement appearance. Double-click items and press P to mark for removal.

## 11. Don't Ignore Injuries

Treat injuries promptly to prevent gangrene and death. Lost colonists mean lost skills and experience.
