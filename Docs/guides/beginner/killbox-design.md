# Tips For Making The Best Killbox In RimWorld

A killbox is a defensive structure designed to funnel raiders into a concentrated area where your colonists can eliminate them efficiently.

## 1. Utilise The Environment

Incorporate natural features like water, marshes, or caves into your killbox design. Environmental hazards slow enemies down, giving you time to gather your colonists and prepare to defend.

## 2. Use Fire To Trap And Kill Raiders

Create non-flammable corridors with flammable objects inside. Close the doors behind enemies once they enter, and set everything on fire with a Molotov. Note: this method yields no loot.

## 3. Place Turrets In The Killbox

Position turrets on killbox sides with wall protection around them. This configuration reduces explosion chain-reaction risks and keeps your pawns safe from turret detonations.

## 4. Use Melee Killboxes For Brawling Colonists

Position three melee fighters — one center, one on each side — at the killbox entrance. Enemies streaming in slowly encounter all three melee guards at once.

## 5. The Killbox Shape Can Help Colonists Fight Back

Semicircle or funnel designs allow enemies entry from one point while providing your pawns a wide area to shoot from, maximizing defensive firepower and shooting angles.

## 6. Slow Enemies Down With Obstacles

Use sandbags or rock chunks on alternating tiles to slow enemy advancement. Obstacles on every tile from the last turn of the corridor to the entryway prevent enemies from using walls as cover.

## 7. Place Traps Inside The Corridors

Position traps throughout snaking corridors to weaken the first wave of attackers. Cheap wooden and steel traps are best for this setup since they're inexpensive and won't damage surrounding walls.

## 8. Use Long Snaked Corridors

Create single-tile-wide winding passages that force enemies into single-file lines. This arrangement allows you to pick them off one by one as they enter.

## 9. Understanding Enemy Pathing

Enemies will generally try to enter your base through the path of least resistance. Keep your front door open to naturally funnel invaders through your prepared defenses rather than having them bash random wall sections.

## 10. Understanding Collision

Combat forces individual units to separate from packed formations. Position a pawn near the entrance so enemies spread out before reaching your base interior, preventing them from overwhelming your defenses all at once.

---

## Summary: Killbox Design Principles

- **Funnel** enemies through a single controlled entry point
- **Layer** traps → obstacles → turrets → colonist firing positions
- **Shape** (semicircle/funnel) maximizes colonist firing angles
- **Single-file corridors** (1-tile wide, snaked) slow and isolate enemies
- **Environment** (water, marshes) and terrain features augment static defenses
- **Turret placement** behind walls prevents chain explosions
- **Fire killboxes** are efficient but yield no loot
