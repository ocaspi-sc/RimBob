# RimWorld Beginner's Guide: Setup, First Steps, New Player Tips

## Creating a New Colony

### Starting Scenarios

**Crashlanded** (Recommended for beginners)
Three colonists, abundant resources, and moderate technology access.

**Lost Tribe**
Five colonists with minimal technological understanding. Significantly more challenging.

**The Rich Explorer**
Single wealthy colonist with many valuable starting items. Difficult due to solo nature.

**Naked Brutality**
Single colonist starts with nothing. Not recommended for beginners.

### AI Storyteller Selection

**Cassandra Classic**
Continuously scaling events that intensify throughout gameplay. Deceptively difficult.

**Phoebe Chillax** (Recommended for first playthrough)
Events scale gradually with adequate recovery time between challenges.

**Randy Random** (Community favorite)
Unpredictable events ranging from immediate devastation to years of peace.

### Difficulty Settings
"Adventure Story" is recommended for beginners instead of the standard "Strive to Survive."

### World & Starting Site Selection
Select a mountainous temperate forest location with granite and marble stone types for optimal resource variety.

### Character Creation

**Recommended Passions**
- Plants (high priority)
- Medicine (high priority)
- Construction (minimum level 5)

**Traits to Avoid**
- Incapable of Dumb Labor
- Incapable of Violence
- Incapable of Hauling
- Serious Health Issues

## First Steps

### Step 1: Equip Weapons
Select colonists and right-click weapons to arm them immediately against potential enemies.

### Step 2: Work Priorities
Enable these for all colonists via the work tab:
- Firefight
- Patient
- Clean
- Haul
- Any major passions (two-flame skills)

### Step 3: Shelter
Construct walls using the Architect menu. A cave or mountainside base works effectively; colonists automatically clear areas and build roofs.

### Step 4: Stockpiles
Create indoor stockpiles through Architect → Zone → Stockpile to prevent resource decay.

### Step 5: Furniture
Build these priority items:
- Torches
- Beds
- Tables
- Chairs
- Chess table

Use Architect → Orders to cut trees if wood becomes scarce.

### Step 6: Farming
Locate fertile soil and create two growing zones of at least 20 tiles each. Plant rice as your primary crop with potatoes or corn as secondary options.

### Step 7: Food Storage
Establish a dedicated food storage room near farms. Use shelves rather than stockpiles, configured to accept only food items. Include at least one wall bordering the outdoors for future freezer conversion.

### Step 8: Kitchen
Attach the kitchen to food storage in a separate room for sanitation. Build a fueled stove and set cooking bills to "Do Until You Have X" (recommend 10 simple meals) for continuous food production.

## Recommended Construction Order

1. Warehouse and garbage dump
2. Five medium-sized fields (two for cotton/medicine, three for food crops: corn, potatoes, rice)
3. Roofed camp with surrounding walls
4. Dwellings with sleeping areas, holding cell, and sick room
5. At least two solar panels with batteries
6. Cold storage house
7. Kitchen with stove and butcher's bench
