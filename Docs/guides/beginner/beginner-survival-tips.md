# RimWorld Beginner's Guide (Version 1.4, 2023)

## Overview

RimWorld is a colony management simulator blending "the Sims and Starcraft," emphasizing storytelling over strict optimization. Players manage colonists, construct bases, defend against threats, and navigate survival on a hostile rim-world planet.

## Core Concepts

### Colonists (Pawns)
Characters possess individual skills (0-20 scale), traits, and needs. They autonomously manage basic functions like eating and sleeping but require task assignments for work. Skills improve through practice and are influenced by passion levels.

### Storytellers
AI systems controlling event frequency and difficulty:
- **Cassandra Classic**: Balanced difficulty with steady escalation
- **Phoebe Chillax**: Longer gaps between major events; potentially deceptive difficulty
- **Randy Random**: Chaotic events; simultaneous challenges possible

Difficulty settings range from Peaceful to Losing is Fun, adjustable at any time.

## Early Game Setup

### Landing Site Selection
Ideal starting locations feature:
- Temperate forest biome
- Year-round growing period
- Nearby hills for building material
- Medium map size (275×275)

### Colonist Selection
Prioritize avoiding these traits:
- Incapable of Dumb/Smart/Violent/Firefighting/Hauling/Cleaning labor
- Pyromaniac trait (automatic disqualification)
- Active addiction or missing limbs

Essential skills to distribute:
- **Medicine**: Minimum one colonist with 8+ skill
- **Construction**: Passion important; minimum 5 skill
- **Plants**: 8+ skill preferred
- **Cooking**: 7+ skill recommended

### Day One Construction
Build a 12×12 room containing:
- Three beds for colonists
- One table with chairs
- Recreation items (horseshoe pin, chess table)
- Indoor stockpile (low priority)

Plant crops in rich soil:
- **Rice**: Quick growth, high labor requirement (best early crop)
- **Corn**: Slower growth, sustainable long-term
- **Potatoes**: Poor soil tolerance
- **Strawberries**: Edible raw (minimal practical use with nutrient dispensers)

Initial area: approximately 100 tiles, expanding 20-25 tiles per new colonist.

## Critical Infrastructure

### Freezer Construction
Temperature-controlled food storage prevents spoilage:
- Build 12×12 room with two coolers
- Set first cooler to -6°C, second to -5°C
- Construct airlock doors to minimize heat transfer
- Connect to power source (windmill or generator)

Power coolers require electrical infrastructure—windmills provide early-game solution despite weather dependency.

### Work Priorities System
Optional but recommended management tool:
- Assign colonists to tasks using numerical priority (lower number = higher priority)
- Set all colonists to Priority 1 for: Firefighting, Patient, Bed Rest, Basic
- Distribute specialized tasks (Construction, Growing, Cooking) among best-skilled individuals
- Reserve Smith, Tailor, Craft, and Art for single expert colonists

### Meal Production
Electric stove requirements:
- Simple meals: basic nutrition without mood penalty
- Fine meals: increased happiness but require meat + vegetables
- Set bills to "Do until you have X" (suggest 5 per colonist)
- Butcher table processes animal corpses into meat/leather

## Defense Strategy

### Early Raid Response
1. Draft all colonists (two-sword icon)
2. Position ranged fighters together; melee fighter forward
3. Face enemies in open terrain outside base

### Perimeter Defense
- Build stone walls surrounding colony
- Leave single deliberate gap to funnel raids
- Create cover (walls/sandbags) facing gap entrance
- Clear enemy cover zone (trees, rocks)
- Place traps (1-tile spacing) on expected approach paths

Hallways minimum 3-tiles wide for effective combat positioning.

### Prisoner Management
- Construct small prison room with bed
- Capture downed enemies via right-click command
- Access Prisoners tab to recruit or execute
- Medical treatment prerequisite for recruitment

## Mid-Game Progression

### Production Facilities
- **Stonecutter's table**: Converts rock to building materials
- **Tailor bench**: Clothing production (powered preferred)
- **Electric smelter**: Steel production from chunks
- **Crematorium**: Body disposal (requires granite)
- **Art bench**: Statue creation for mood/room quality

### Resource Management
- Replace wooden structures with stone (fire prevention)
- Hunt dangerous animals in groups
- Establish animal breeding for sustainable meat supply
- Hunt rich mineral deposits using scanners
- Deep drill cautiously (infestation risk)

### Advanced Infrastructure
- Geothermal power generators (reliable but require walls, steam vents)
- Multiple batteries (manage ZZZT fire risk)
- Separate production areas with adequate lighting
- Dedicated cleaner for hygiene/efficiency

## Colonist Management

### Mood System
Baseline: 37 (Adventure Story difficulty), adjusting downward as colony prosperity increases.

**Positive modifiers**:
- Excellent food/comfortable/beautiful spaces
- Recreation access
- Social bonds
- Successful combat/inspirations

**Negative modifiers**:
- Low needs (food, rest, recreation)
- Poor conditions
- Witnessing death/violence
- Illness/pain

**Mental breaks** trigger when mood drops below threshold markers:
- Minor: wandering, insulting colonists
- Major: tantrums, corpse desecration
- Extreme: violence, arson, abandonment

Prevent breaks through need fulfillment and environmental quality.

### Recruitment Mechanics
- Captured prisoners become recruitable (social skill affects speed)
- Arriving refugees possible through events
- Most individuals acceptable long-term except extreme cases

## Research Progression
Suggested early research path:
1. **Batteries**: Mitigate windmill inconsistency
2. **Geothermal power**: Reliable energy infrastructure
3. **Stonecutting**: Stone wall replacement
4. **Electronics**: Advanced machinery unlocks

## Common Early Mistakes to Avoid
- Assigning pyromaniac colonists
- Overstocking batteries (fire risk)
- Inadequate doctor coverage (medical backup essential)
- Neglecting colonist mood/needs
- Placing coolers incorrectly (blue side must face inward)
- Building single-tile-wide hallways
- Excessive statue production (wealth scaling increases difficulty)
